## 2. Flaws Identified on Original Listing ("What We Found")
1. **All 8 Photos Are Shared Facilities Only:** None of the uploaded photos show the actual 20th-floor unit (living room, bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, or floor-20 view).
2. **Center-Spanning Watermarks & Low Resolution (800px):** Every photo has a large spaced-out `C H A L I T A   C H A M C H O Y` watermark cutting across the center of the frame, plus `#IamFormThonglor` across the screening room projection screen.
3. **Missing Unit Floor Plan:** The 2 Bedroom M (~63.5 sqm) layout is absent from the gallery.
4. **Distance Typos in Description:** Lists BTS Thong Lo as "1.5 metres" (actually ~1.4–1.5 km) and Bangkok Prep as "3.9 metres" (metres instead of kilometres).
5. **Copy-Pasted Developer Text:** The description mostly pastes generic developer history, sale prices per sqm, and even references another project (*Noble BE33*) instead of clear move-in terms for renters.
